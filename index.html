Difference snapshot

Australia is usually between 17 and 19 hours ahead of California, depending on the destination timezone and daylight saving season.

Working-hours hint

Great overlap usually happens early morning in California and the following early morning to afternoon in Australia.

California

California uses Pacific Time. It switches between PST and PDT depending on daylight saving time, so the Australia offset changes during the year.

Eastern Australia

Sydney and Melbourne usually share the same timezone. Brisbane stays on standard time, which can create a one-hour difference during Australian summer.

Central and west

Adelaide and Darwin are in half-hour based central time zones, while Perth is further west and often the closest Australian match to California business hours.
